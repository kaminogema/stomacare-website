**StomaCare** is an all-in-one daily companion and clinical self-management suite designed specifically for individuals living with a **colostomy**, **ileostomy**, or **urostomy**. 

From hospital discharge to active daily routines, StomaCare provides the exact tools needed to eliminate pouch leaks, diet uncertainty, and travel restroom anxiety.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🚨 Emergency Medical Restroom Pass ("I Can't Wait" Card)
* **Immediate Access**: Formulated according to Restroom Access Act regulations (**Ally's Law**) and medical necessity to prevent humiliating denials at employee-only restrooms.
* **1-Tap 10-Language Switcher**: Instant translation into 10 world languages with dedicated flag buttons for stress-free international travel.
* **Zero-Scroll Clinical Display**: Formatted cleanly to fit 100% within the mobile viewport without requiring scrolling.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Stores patient name, condition, emergency contact phone, and stoma nurse details.

### 📏 Stoma Wafer Cut Sizing Ruler
* **Concentric Guide Rings**: Dual concentric display featuring a solid teal inner circle (stoma base) and an amber dashed outer ring (+1.5mm to +2mm clearance) to prevent caustic peristomal skin burns.
* **Continuous Micro-Slider**: Stepper adjustments from 15mm to 70mm with quick preset chips (22mm, 25mm, 32mm, 38mm).
* **Post-Op Shrinkage Tracker**: Log measurements across weeks 1–8 to monitor healing reduction and share trends with your WOCN nurse.

### 🥑 Intelligent Nutrition & Blockage Prevention Assistant
* **Clinical Meal Analysis**: Deconstructs complex cooked dishes (pizza, pasta, casseroles) into safe vs. high-risk components.
* **Bowel Obstruction Warnings**: Specific alerts for indigestible blockage hazards (raw nuts, mushrooms, corn kernels, fibrous fruit skins).
* **3-Stage Post-Op Diet Protocol**: Automated guidelines transitioning from low-residue gentle foods to balanced long-term nutrition.

### 🚻 Emergency Restroom Radar
* **GPS Proximity Locator**: Live metric walking distance to public, municipal, and commercial facilities worldwide.
* **Wheelchair & Accessibility Filter**: Identifies facilities with grab bars, clean running water, and counter space suitable for sanitary pouch changes.
* **1-Tap Navigation**: Launches directions directly in Google Maps, Apple Maps, or OpenStreetMap.

### 🎒 Departure Go-Bag Checklist
* **Dynamic Readiness Meter**: Visual score calculating bag preparedness before stepping out the door.
* **Supply Inventory Sync**: Alerts when adhesive wafers, drainable pouches, barrier rings, or wipes run low.

### ⏰ Clinical Care Protocols & Timers
* **60s Hydrocolloid Adhesive Warm-Up**: Thermal activation timer ensuring maximum bond strength to prevent blowouts.
* **1/3 Fullness Reminders**: Timely alerts to empty the pouch before weight breaks the seal.

## 🔒 Privacy & Architecture

StomaCare is engineered on a strict **offline-first, privacy-by-design** architecture:
* **Zero Cloud Health Tracking**: All surgery dates, stoma measurements, diary notes, and patient contacts remain exclusively in your phone's local storage (`IndexedDB` / `LocalStorage`).
* **Secure Serverless AI Proxy**: Natural language queries are routed through a secure, rate-limited Cloudflare Worker proxy (`api.stomacare.app`). **No private medical data or API keys are ever stored on the client device.**
* **GDPR & HIPAA Conscious**: We never sell or share user data with advertisers, insurance carriers, or data brokers.
