Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 19, 2026
Privacy-First Guarantee: StomaCare was engineered from the ground up to protect ostomates. Your health diary entries, stoma sizes, and emergency medical contacts are stored strictly on your local device. We never sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

The StomaCare mobile application operates primarily on an offline-first architecture to protect sensitive health metrics:

  • Health Diary & Logged Metrics: Your stoma diameter history, wafer sizing logs, bag change timestamps, hydration tracking, and skin inspection notes are stored solely inside your phone's local storage (IndexedDB/LocalStorage). This data never leaves your device unless you manually export or backup your phone.
  • Emergency Restroom Pass Data: Patient name, surgery condition, emergency telephone numbers, and physician contacts inputted into the Emergency Pass modal remain securely on your device.
  • Location Information (Restroom Radar): When you search for nearby accessible or public restrooms, the app requests one-time foreground location permission. Your geographic coordinates (GPS latitude/longitude) are used exclusively to calculate distance to nearby facilities via OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) and are never stored or tracked over time.
  • AI Food Consultation (Gemini API): When using the Food Safety AI Assistant, the text query (e.g., "Can I eat sweetcorn 3 weeks post-op?") is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Google's Gemini API strictly to generate the educational response. No personal medical history, patient names, or contact information are attached to the AI query.

2. Google Play Data Safety Summary

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety Declaration policies:

  • Data Collection: We do NOT collect personally identifiable data (PID) or share health data with third-party advertising or analytics networks.
  • Location: Ephemeral, used only while the Restroom Radar is actively queried.
  • Data Deletion: You have full control over your data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local logs, diary notes, and patient pass details.
  • Security Practices: All network communications (Overpass OpenStreetMap requests and Gemini AI) use industry-standard HTTPS encryption in transit.

3. Medical Disclaimer & Non-Diagnostic Purpose

Important Medical Notice: StomaCare is an educational, self-management, and lifestyle companion. It is NOT a certified medical device and does NOT offer medical diagnosis, clinical treatment plans, or emergency triage.

Always adhere to the specific instructions provided by your colorectal surgeon, certified Wound Ostomy Continence Nurse (WOCN), or gastroenterology clinical team.

If you experience severe abdominal cramping lasting over 2 hours without stoma output, abnormal stoma stoma retraction or prolapse, discoloration (dark purple/black), or persistent bleeding, immediately seek emergency medical attention or contact your hospital stoma helpline.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

StomaCare may provide links to public mapping providers (OpenStreetMap, Google Maps, Apple Maps) for restroom turn-by-turn navigation. Please consult their respective privacy policies when opening external navigation apps.
