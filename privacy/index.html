Zero-Cloud Medical Privacy

Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 20, 2026
Privacy-First Guarantee: StomaCare was created with a zero-knowledge architecture. We do NOT require user accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords. 100% of your personal health information remains exclusively on your physical device.

1. Google Play Data Safety Disclosure

In compliance with Google Play Store policies and international medical data protection frameworks (including GDPR, HIPAA guidelines, and CCPA):

  • Data Collected: None. StomaCare does not collect, harvest, transmit, or monetize any user data.
  • Data Shared: None. Zero personal data or device identifiers are shared with third parties, analytics companies, or advertising networks.
  • Security Practices: All patient data entered into the app (such as surgery date, stoma wafer measurements, hydration intake, and pouch change reminders) is stored locally using sandboxed Web Storage (LocalStorage and IndexedDB).

2. What Information StomaCare Processes Locally

All of the following information is processed strictly on your device and never leaves your phone:

  • Surgical & Health Timeline: Surgery date, recovery stage, and days post-operation.
  • Wafer Sizing Data: Digital ring measurements (mm), photos taken through your device camera for personal progression monitoring, and leak incident logs.
  • Emergency Pass Information: Patient name, physician contact, and emergency phone numbers entered into the Medical Restroom Pass card.
  • Nutrition & Diet Queries: When searching for foods and blockage risks, queries are answered using the embedded on-device food risk database or processed through our secured server proxy without storing identity.

3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Used

StomaCare requests only the minimum necessary permissions required for clinical features to operate:

  • Camera / Storage (Optional): Required only if you choose to take or upload a photo of your stoma to monitor peristomal skin recovery over time. Photos remain inside your device's isolated storage and are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Location / GPS (Optional): Used exclusively within the Emergency Restroom Radar to calculate distances to nearby public toilets via OpenStreetMap. Your coordinates are never tracked, logged, or saved.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used locally to chime reminders for pouch changes and water intake.

4. Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

StomaCare contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero commercial trackers, and zero marketing pixels. Our website uses privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Plausible) solely to measure aggregate page visits without collecting cookies or IP addresses.

5. Medical Disclaimer

StomaCare is designed for daily self-management and educational assistance. It is not a certified medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always seek the advice of your colorectal surgeon, Stoma Care Nurse (SCN), or Wound Ostomy Continence Nurse (WOCN) regarding any medical concerns or complications.
